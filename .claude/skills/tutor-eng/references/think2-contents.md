# Think 2 (2nd edition) — зміст

**Welcome** (p. 4)
- **A** Introducing yourself; Asking questions; The weather; Families
- **B** Meeting people; Irregular past participles; Losing things; Furniture
- **C** Buying and talking about food; In a restaurant; Shops; Things you have to do
- **D** Plans and arrangements; Sports and sport verbs; Travel plans

## Unit 1 — Incredible people (p. 12)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Talking about things you have and haven't done; feelings and personality; hobbies and interests; physical characteristics and appearance. Developing Speaking: Offering encouragement
- **Grammar:** Present perfect with *just*, *already* and *yet*; Present perfect vs. past simple
- **Vocabulary:** Personality adjectives; Collocations; WordWise: Phrases with *just*
- **Pronunciation:** Intonation and sentence stress
- **Think!:** Values: Human qualities
- **Skills:** Reading — Online survey responses: Who do you admire most?; Article: Human moles. Writing — A short passage about someone you admire. Listening — Guessing game

## Unit 2 — A good education (p. 20)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Asking and giving / refusing permission to do something; Talking about school life and education. Role play: Asking permission
- **Grammar:** Present perfect with *for* and *since*; *a*, *an*, *the* or no article
- **Vocabulary:** School subjects; Verbs about thinking
- **Pronunciation:** Word stress
- **Think!:** Values: Learning for life; Train to Think: Learning about texts
- **Skills:** Reading — Article: The loneliest schoolboy in Britain; Article: The power of 'yet'. Writing — An email describing your school routine. Listening — Conversation about a summer activity list

*Life competencies:* Recognising others' good work · *Culture:* Schools with a difference · Review · B1 Preliminary for Schools Exam practice

## Unit 3 — On the screen (p. 30)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Comparing things and actions. Developing Speaking: Asking for and offering help
- **Grammar:** Comparative and superlative adjectives (review); *(not) as … as* comparatives; Making a comparison stronger or weaker; Adverbs and comparative adverbs; Order of adjectives; adverbs of degree (p. 133)
- **Vocabulary:** Types of films; WordWise: Expressions with *get*; Types of TV programmes
- **Pronunciation:** Words ending in /ə/
- **Think!:** Values: Preconceived ideas
- **Skills:** Reading — Article: A new kind of superhero; Article: The history of watching TV. Writing — A paragraph about your TV habits. Listening — Interview with a teenage filmmaker

## Unit 4 — Online life (p. 38)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Giving advice, talking about technology and ways of communicating and socialising
- **Grammar:** Indefinite pronouns (*everyone*, *no one*, *someone*, etc.); *all / some / none / any of them*; *should(n't)*, *had better*, *ought to*; modal verbs in the past tense (p. 134)
- **Vocabulary:** IT terms; Language for giving advice
- **Pronunciation:** The short /ʌ/ vowel sound
- **Think!:** Values: Responsible online behaviour; Train to Think: Logical sequencing
- **Skills:** Reading — Article: Leaving footprints; Short texts: Different types of messages. Writing — A web page giving advice. Listening — Conversation about falling for online scams

*Life competencies:* Achieving your goals · *Culture:* From caves to Kindles – how reading has changed · Review · B1 Preliminary for Schools Exam practice

## Unit 5 — Music to my ears (p. 48)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Role play: Helpful suggestions talking about free time activities. Developing Speaking: Asking about feelings
- **Grammar:** Present perfect continuous; Present perfect simple vs. present perfect continuous
- **Vocabulary:** Making music; Musical instruments; WordWise: Phrasal verbs with *out*
- **Pronunciation:** *been*: strong /biːn/ and weak /bɪn/
- **Think!:** Values: Following your dreams
- **Skills:** Reading — Magazine article: A bright star; Article: The future of live music. Writing — The story of your favourite band. Listening — Interviews about music

## Unit 6 — No planet B (p. 56)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Expressing surprise and enthusiasm
- **Grammar:** *will (not)*, *may (not)*, *might (not)* for prediction; First conditional; *unless* in first conditional sentences
- **Vocabulary:** The environment; Verbs to talk about energy
- **Pronunciation:** /f/, /v/, /b/ consonant sounds
- **Think!:** Values: Caring for the world; Train to Think: Different perspectives
- **Skills:** Reading — Magazine article: Hot topic: saving the Earth; Leaflet: Energy matters. Writing — An article for the school magazine. Listening — Interview about a town project

*Life competencies:* Managing your money · *Culture:* Green solutions around the world · Review · B1 Preliminary for Schools Exam practice

## Unit 7 — The future is now (p. 66)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Checking information. Developing Speaking: Agreeing
- **Grammar:** Future forms; Question tags; *Nor*, *neither* and *so*; future continuous tense (p. 130 — номер сторінки на фото нечіткий)
- **Vocabulary:** Future time expressions; Arranging a party; WordWise: Phrases with *about*
- **Pronunciation:** Intonation of question tags
- **Think!:** Values: Believe in a better future
- **Skills:** Reading — Newspaper article: Today's world; Group chat: Arranging a party. Writing — An invitation to a party. Listening — Interview with a singer and a journalist

## Unit 8 — Science and us (p. 74)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Talking about past habits; Talking about imaginary situations; Talking about scientific discoveries; Talking about education
- **Grammar:** Past simple vs. past continuous (review); *used to*; Second conditional; *I wish*
- **Vocabulary:** Direction and movement; Science
- **Pronunciation:** The /juː/ sound
- **Think!:** Values: How science helps people; Train to Think: Using criteria
- **Skills:** Reading — Magazine article: Curious minds; Web forum: What invention or discovery would make your life better? Writing — A blog entry. Listening — A science lesson

*Life competencies:* Being flexible · *Culture:* Amazing inventions from ancient civilizations · Review · B1 Preliminary for Schools Exam practice

## Unit 9 — Working week (p. 84)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Developing Speaking: Accepting and refusing offers and invitations. Role play: Inviting friends to join you
- **Grammar:** The passive (present simple, past simple, present continuous, present perfect)
- **Vocabulary:** Jobs; *work as / in / for*; *work* vs. *job*; WordWise: Time expressions with *in*
- **Pronunciation:** /tʃ/ and /dʒ/ consonant sounds
- **Think!:** Values: What's important in a job?
- **Skills:** Reading — Article: The perfect job?; Article: Technology in the workplace. Writing — A short essay about jobs in the future. Listening — Interview with Marina Stuart

## Unit 10 — Mind and body (p. 92)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Talking about your health; Talking about different lifestyles
- **Grammar:** Past perfect simple; Negative and interrogative sentences (p. 136); Past perfect continuous; Past perfect simple vs. past perfect continuous
- **Vocabulary:** Time linkers; Illness: collocations; Lifestyle
- **Pronunciation:** /tʃ/ and /ʃ/ consonant sounds
- **Think!:** Values: Never give up; Train to Think: Logical conclusions
- **Skills:** Reading — Article: 8,000 birds to see before you die; Article: 3D printing – it can change lives. Writing — A story. Listening — Conversation about health and exercise

*Life competencies:* Taking responsibility for your actions · *Culture:* Keep in shape … without going to the gym · Review · B1 Preliminary for Schools Exam practice

## Unit 11 — Breaking news (p. 102)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Reporting what someone has said. Developing Speaking: Expressing feelings: anger
- **Grammar:** Reported statements; Verb patterns: object + infinitive
- **Vocabulary:** Fun; More verbs with object + infinitive; WordWise: Expressions with *make*
- **Pronunciation:** Intonation: rude or polite?
- **Think!:** Values: Reacting to jokes
- **Skills:** Reading — Article: It's all fake news; Article: The danger of live TV. Writing — A news report. Listening — Profile of an extreme weather journalist

## Unit 12 — Rules and regulations (p. 110)
- **Functions & Speaking:** Talking about permission; Following and giving simple instructions
- **Grammar:** *be allowed to / let*; Third conditional
- **Vocabulary:** Discipline; Consequences and reasons
- **Pronunciation:** Silent consonants
- **Think!:** Values: The importance of rules; Train to Think: Being creative
- **Skills:** Reading — Article: Hard times to be a kid; Website contest: The best 50-word stories. Writing — A set of rules. Listening — The rules of chess

*Life competencies:* Being assertive · *Culture:* Strange laws from around the world · Review · B1 Preliminary for Schools Exam practice
